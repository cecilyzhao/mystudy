1 Core Concept — 30 Seconds

▼
🔑 One-Line Summary
Stoichiometry uses the coefficients of a balanced chemical equation as mole ratios to convert between amounts of reactants and products.
🧠 What Balanced Equations Tell Us
Tells us: reactants, products, mole ratios, physical states
Does NOT tell us: colour, shape, density, solubility, rate, or how the reaction looks
nrequired = ngiven × (coefficientrequired / coefficientgiven)
📌 Reaction Assumptions
1. Reactions are spontaneous and fast
2. Reactions are stoichiometric (whole-number ratios)
3. Reactions are quantitative (≥99% complete — at least one reactant fully used up)

2 Method — Using Mole Ratios

▼
📋 Steps to Solve Mole Ratio Problems
Write and balance the chemical equation
Identify the given substance and the required substance
Use the mole ratio (coefficients) to convert:
nrequired = ngiven × (coeffrequired / coeffgiven)
⚠️ Sig Dig Rule for Mole Ratios
Mole ratios are exact (whole number ratios from balanced equations). When multiplying by a whole-number ratio, treat it like repeated addition — maintain the number of decimal places, not sig digs.
🧠 Example Ratios — N₂ + 3 H₂ → 2 NH₃
Overall: 1 N₂ : 3 H₂ : 2 NH₃
H₂ to NH₃ ratio: 3 mol H₂ : 2 mol NH₃
N₂ to NH₃ ratio: 1 mol N₂ : 2 mol NH₃

3 Must-Know Problem — Full Worked Example

▼
📝 Malachite Decomposition

Given: 0.0045 mol of Cu₂(CO₃)(OH)₂ decomposes completely. How many mol of CuO are produced?

Balanced equation:

Cu₂(CO₃)(OH)₂(s) → 2 CuO(s) + CO₂(g) + H₂O(g)

Identify the mole ratio:

1 mol Cu₂(CO₃)(OH)₂ : 2 mol CuO

Calculate:

nCuO = 0.0045 mol × (2 mol CuO / 1 mol Cu₂(CO₃)(OH)₂)
nCuO = 0.0090 mol CuO
📝 Combustion of Octane

Write the ratio for complete combustion of octane:

2 C₈H₁₈(ℓ) + 25 O₂(g) → 16 CO₂(g) + 18 H₂O(g)
Overall ratio: 2 C₈H₁₈ : 25 O₂ : 16 CO₂ : 18 H₂O

4 Quick Check — 3 Questions

▼

Q1: For 2 H₂ + O₂ → 2 H₂O, what is the mole ratio of O₂ to H₂O?

✅ Correct! 1 mol O₂ : 2 mol H₂O from the coefficients.
❌ Look at the coefficients: 1 O₂ produces 2 H₂O.

Q2: N₂ + 3 H₂ → 2 NH₃
How many moles of NH₃ from 4.5 mol H₂?

✅ Correct! 4.5 mol H₂ × (2 mol NH₃ / 3 mol H₂) = 3.0 mol NH₃
❌ Use the ratio: 4.5 × (2/3) = 3.0 mol NH₃

Q3: 2 C₂H₆ + 7 O₂ → 4 CO₂ + 6 H₂O
What is the mole ratio of ethane to O₂?

✅ Correct! Directly from the coefficients: 2 C₂H₆ : 7 O₂
❌ Read the coefficients directly: 2 mol C₂H₆ : 7 mol O₂

5 Practice Problem — Challenge Yourself

▼

🎯 Challenge: Ammonium Sulfate Production

The fertilizer ammonium sulfate is made at Fort Saskatchewan:

2 NH₃(g) + H₂SO₄(aq) → (NH₄)₂SO₄(s)

a) Write the ratio of all components.

b) What amount of NH₃ is needed to make 20,000 mol of (NH₄)₂SO₄?

a) Ratio: 2 NH₃ : 1 H₂SO₄ : 1 (NH₄)₂SO₄

b) Moles of NH₃:
nNH₃ = 20,000 mol (NH₄)₂SO₄ × (2 mol NH₃ / 1 mol (NH₄)₂SO₄)
nNH₃ = 40,000 mol NH₃

🎯 Bonus: Silver Chromate

2 AgNO₃(aq) + Na₂CrO₄(aq) → Ag₂CrO₄(s) + 2 NaNO₃(aq)

a) Write the mole ratio of AgNO₃ to Ag₂CrO₄.

b) What amount of Ag₂CrO₄ is produced from 0.50 mol AgNO₃?

a) 2 mol AgNO₃ : 1 mol Ag₂CrO₄

b)
nAg₂CrO₄ = 0.50 mol AgNO₃ × (1 mol Ag₂CrO₄ / 2 mol AgNO₃)
nAg₂CrO₄ = 0.25 mol Ag₂CrO₄
